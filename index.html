About SEED Lab

The Soil Ecology and Environmental Dynamics (SEED) Lab investigates how soil biogeochemical processes respond to global environmental change. Our research integrates field experiments, laboratory analyses, and quantitative approaches to understand soil carbon cycling, ecosystem functioning, and climate–soil feedbacks across terrestrial ecosystems. Our goal is to identify nature-based solutions to carbon sequestration, climate mitigation, and food security.

Principal Investigator

Guopeng Liang
Assistant Professor

Research Experience

Guopeng received a Ph.D. degree in Dr. Bonnie Waring's lab at Utah State University. Prior to joining the University of Hong Kong, he conducted postdoc research with Dr. Peter Reich at the University of Minnesota and Dr. Michelle Wong at Yale University. He has published 15 papers as a first or sole author in high-impact journals, including Nature Geoscience, Nature Reviews Biodiversity, Nature Communications, Global Change Biology, Global Ecology and Biogeography, and Global Food Security. Guopeng serves as an associate editor or editorial board member for eight journals (e.g., Ecological Monographs, Global Ecology and Biogeography, New Phytologist, Communications Earth and Environment, Carbon Balance and Management, Plant and Soil, Soil Use and Management, and Applied Soil Ecology).
